FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Find answers to common questions

What is the minimum order quantity? ▼

Our minimum order quantity varies by product type. For custom jerseys, we start at 10 pieces. For corporate apparel, we can accommodate orders as low as 25 pieces.

How long does delivery take? ▼

Standard delivery takes 7-14 business days for custom orders. Express delivery is available for an additional fee with 3-5 business days turnaround.

Do you offer international shipping? ▼

Yes! We ship worldwide. International delivery times vary from 14-30 business days depending on the destination.

Can I get a sample before bulk order? ▼

Absolutely! We offer sample production for custom orders. Sample fee is refundable on bulk orders above 50 pieces.

What payment methods do you accept? ▼

We accept all major credit cards, bank transfers, and mobile banking (bKash, Nagad). For bulk orders, we also offer payment in installments.
